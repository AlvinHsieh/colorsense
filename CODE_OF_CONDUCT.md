# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in the ColorSense community a harassment-free experience for
everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex
characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic
status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and
orientation.

## Our standards

Positive behavior includes empathy, constructive feedback, respect for differing experiences, and
accepting responsibility. Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, personal attacks, trolling,
sexualized attention, publishing private information, or conduct inappropriate in a professional
setting.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately through the repository owner's GitHub profile contact channel.
The maintainer may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, Issues, and other contributions
that violate this policy, and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation.

Enforcement decisions should be proportionate, documented privately, and protect reporter privacy.
